# RIMWAKE local demonstration

Open http://127.0.0.1:4175 . This is the tested production preview, not a public deployment.

1. **0-5 seconds:** Show the lunar opening: where you stand changes your view. Use the header Explorer link for immediate interaction.
2. **5-13 seconds:** Choose **Explore a real observation**. Advance to **Read the difference**. At 14 November 00:00 UTC the Sun is below the plane at A and above at B; Earth has the opposite classification. Both panels use the same UTC.
3. **13-22 seconds:** Exit discovery, choose **Follow a crossing**, advance to its comparison, then move the clock. These are actual 03:30 and 03:40 samples on 16 November. The exact crossing time remains bracketed.
4. **22-27 seconds:** Inspect evidence: hypothetical observer definition, JPL requests, hashes and limitations.
5. **27-30 seconds:** State the value: a geographic and temporal comparison that makes source-backed geometry easier to inspect. Terrain, power and communication performance require further models.

For a longer review, use the map picker, find a matching sampled run, copy the observation link, refresh it, and download the selected four-row CSV. Check the same observation in calibrated SVG view or with reduced motion.

The six-slide HTML/PDF is an alternative local demo, not a submitted artifact. The current official guide permits up to seven slides including the title or a video of at most 30 seconds. Team/member information and public links require owner completion before submission.
